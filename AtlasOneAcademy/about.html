Who We Are

A community built on care and high standards

Atlas One Academy serves students from Preschool through Junior High School (JHS), with a curriculum that balances strong academics, character formation, and room for every child to find what they're good at.

Mission

To provide quality, values-based education that prepares every child for the next stage of life.

Vision

To be a leading academy known for academic excellence and well-rounded graduates.

Discipline

A structured, respectful environment where students are held to clear expectations.

Community

Small class sizes so every student is known by name, not just a number.

Levels Offered

  • Preschool — ages 3–5, play-based early learning
  • Lower Primary — Basic 1–3, core literacy & numeracy
  • Upper Primary — Basic 4–6, expanded subjects & projects
  • Junior High School — Basic 7–9, BECE preparation
