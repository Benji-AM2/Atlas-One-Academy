Admissions Process

A straightforward four-step process from inquiry to enrollment.

1

Inquiry

Submit an inquiry form or visit the office to learn about openings.

2

Application

Complete and submit the application form with required documents.

3

Assessment

Student completes a brief placement assessment and family interview.

4

Enrollment

Confirm placement, complete fees, and begin orientation.
